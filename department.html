Our Hospital Departments

Cardiology

Our Cardiology Department specializes in heart-related conditions, providing advanced diagnostics, treatments, and surgeries. Our team of expert cardiologists uses the latest technology to treat heart diseases, including heart attacks, arrhythmias, and heart failure.

Neurology

The Neurology Department is dedicated to treating disorders of the nervous system, including strokes, epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, and more. Our neurologists offer both diagnostic and therapeutic services using state-of-the-art equipment.

Pediatrics

The Pediatrics Department is committed to providing compassionate care to children, from infancy through adolescence. We offer general pediatric care, immunizations, and treatments for childhood diseases.

Orthopedics

Our Orthopedics Department provides care for musculoskeletal injuries and conditions, including fractures, joint replacement, and spine surgery. We aim to help patients recover mobility and improve their quality of life.

Dermatology

Our Dermatology Department specializes in diagnosing and treating skin conditions such as acne, eczema, psoriasis, and skin cancer. Our dermatologists provide both medical and cosmetic treatments to improve skin health.

General Surgery

Our General Surgery Department provides a wide range of surgical services, from minimally invasive procedures to complex surgeries. We focus on patient recovery and offer state-of-the-art surgical techniques.

Oncology

Our Oncology Department specializes in cancer diagnosis and treatment, including chemotherapy, radiation therapy, and surgical oncology. Our multidisciplinary team works to provide personalized care for every patient.

Radiology

The Radiology Department offers advanced imaging services such as MRI, CT scans, X-rays, and ultrasounds to aid in accurate diagnosis and treatment planning.

Urology

Our Urology Department provides expert care for urinary tract and male reproductive system issues, including kidney stones, prostate problems, and urological cancers.

Ophthalmology

The Ophthalmology Department focuses on eye health, offering treatments for cataracts, glaucoma, macular degeneration, and corrective eye surgeries.

Psychiatry

Our Psychiatry Department is committed to mental health care, offering therapy and treatment for conditions such as anxiety, depression, and bipolar disorder.

Gastroenterology

The Gastroenterology Department provides care for digestive system disorders, including conditions like GERD, ulcers, and liver diseases.

Surgery

The Surgery Department provides expertise in performing a wide range of surgeries, including emergency, trauma, and elective procedures, with a focus on patient safety and recovery.

Pulmonary

Our Pulmonary Department is dedicated to diagnosing and treating lung and respiratory disorders such as asthma, COPD, pneumonia, and sleep apnea, using cutting-edge technology.

Gynecology

The Gynecology Department offers comprehensive care for women's health, including reproductive system disorders, prenatal care, and menopause management.

Immunology

The Immunology Department specializes in diagnosing and treating immune system disorders, including allergies, autoimmune diseases, and immunodeficiency conditions.

Hepatology

Our Hepatology Department provides advanced care for liver-related conditions, such as hepatitis, cirrhosis, and liver cancer, with state-of-the-art diagnostic and treatment facilities.

Geriatrics

The Geriatrics Department focuses on the unique health needs of older adults, offering services for age-related conditions, memory care, and mobility support.

Medicine

The Medicine Department provides primary and specialty care for various conditions, focusing on accurate diagnosis, treatment, and patient-centered care.

Allergology

The Allergology Department specializes in diagnosing and treating allergic conditions such as hay fever, food allergies, drug allergies, and eczema, using advanced testing and treatment options.

Anesthesiology

The Anesthesiology Department provides expert care in pain management and anesthesia for surgical and diagnostic procedures, ensuring patient safety and comfort during operations.
